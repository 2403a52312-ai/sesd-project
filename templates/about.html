ABOUT THE PROJECT

Helping job seekers spot warning signs.

JobGuard AI is an academic project that applies Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning to text-based job-posting analysis.

How it works

01

Enter the posting

Paste the job or internship description into the checker.

02

Process the text

TF-IDF converts the text into numerical features for the model.

03

Predict and review

The trained classifier estimates the likelihood of a fraudulent label and displays warning indicators.

Important: This tool cannot independently verify a company, recruiter, website or job offer. Always check the employer's official website and contact details. Never pay a fee to obtain a job.
